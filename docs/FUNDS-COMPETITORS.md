# Who else is doing this

October 2026. Players working on the same leaks as Axolotl's fund product
(`FUNDS-WRONG-PAYER.md`), grouped by how close they are. Sources inline.
*Inferred* marks my reading.

## Closest: they already sell "make Medicare pay first" to plan sponsors

These are the real competitors for leaks #1–5: Medicare enrollment, Social
Security disability (SSDI) and Medicare coordination.

**SSDC Services Corp.** (Farmington Hills, MI, founded 1979)
- **What it does:** SSDI awards plus "Medicare Coordination Services" for
  organizations. Free to the member.
- **Claims:** 100,000+ SSDI awards, a 97% award rate, 300,000+ employees and
  retirees enrolled in Medicare, and "billions" in health plan savings.
- **Clients:**
  - Boeing retirees
  - New Jersey's state and school employee health plans (SHBP/SEHBP), for
    pre-65 retirees
  - Colorado PERA
- **Sources:** [NJ DPB notice](https://nj.gov/treasury/pensions/documents/hb/oe2021/SSDC-NJDPB.pdf),
  [Boeing / BCBSIL](https://www.bcbsil.com/boeing/additional-benefits/medicare),
  [Colorado PERA](https://secure.copera.org/resources/newsletters/retiree-report/fall-2020/new-way-get-help-social-security-disability-insurance),
  [Shortlister](https://www.myshortlister.com/ssdc-services/vendor-reviews)

**Allsup**
- **What it does:**
  - SSDI representation (400,000+ people helped)
  - "Medicare Coordination": it files the CMS payer-status update so the plan
    becomes secondary
  - "Medicare Verification": it enrolls retirees in Parts A and B during
    retiree plan transitions
- **Sold to:** insurers, employers and plan sponsors.
- **Sources:** [Plansponsor](https://www.plansponsor.com/allsup-helps-coordinate-medicare-for-employees-and-retirees/),
  [Allsup HIS overview](https://www.allsup.com/media/files/HIS-overview.pdf),
  [Allsup Benefits Coordination](https://benefits.ryansg.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Allsup-Benefits-Coordination-Services.pdf)

**Advocator Group** (Brown & Brown)
- **What it does:** SSDI advocacy, with 200,000+ awards and initial approval
  rates "nearly double the national average," plus Medicare guidance.
- **Sold to:** 18 of the top 20 disability carriers.
- **Source:** [Brown & Brown Absence Services](https://www.bbabsence.com/ssdi-advocacy-services/)

**Public Consulting Group**
- **What it does:** Connecticut's state retirees: SSDI application help,
  Medicare enrollment for pre-65 retirees, and Part B/D reimbursement data.
  Bought by RFP.
- **Sources:** [CT RFP](https://osc.ct.gov:443/vendor/rfps/2023/RFP%20-%20Social%20Security%20Disability%20Application%20Assistance.pdf),
  [CT FAQ](https://osc.ct.gov/faqs/can-i-get-help-with-my-application-for-social-security-disability-benefits/)

**What this means**
- The disability and Medicare-coordination leaks are not empty. They're
  sold, they're proven, and big public plans and Boeing buy them.
- I found no evidence that these firms serve union (Taft-Hartley) funds, but
  that's absence of evidence, not proof.
- All four work by phone and paper case management on one program. None
  covers the Medicare Savings Program / Extra Help, other-coverage or
  life-event leaks.
- **Our site's "each piece is sold to someone else" line is too strong.**
  SSDC sells the disability piece to employers and public retiree plans
  directly.

## Close on public-benefit enrollment, different buyer

**DUOS** ($33M+ raised)
- **What it does:** AI plus human navigation that enrolls older adults in
  SNAP, Medicaid, VA and state benefits.
- **Sold to:** Medicare Advantage and dual-eligible health plans, plus
  providers.
- **Users:** 88% earn under $28,000 a year, and the average age is 74.
- **Not sold to:** union funds or employers.
- **Sources:** [DUOS](https://getduos.com/medicare-advantage-plans),
  [Home Health Care News](https://homehealthcarenews.com/2023/06/senior-care-startup-duos-raises-10m-bringing-its-funding-total-to-more-than-33m/)

**BeneStream**
- **What it does:** Medicaid, CHIP and ACA screening and enrollment for
  low-wage workers.
- **Sold to:** employers, at $40 per screen plus $20 a month per enrollee.
- **Size:** small (about $1.6M raised). Now part of ful. Health's "CashPay"
  ecosystem.
- **Sources:** [KFF Health News](https://kffhealthnews.org/health-industry/might-your-workers-be-eligible-for-medicaid-start-up-helps-employers-find-out/),
  [press release](https://webdisclosure.com/press-release/ful-health-nasdaq-ful-cashpay-welcomes-benestream-to-its-healthcare-ecosystem-providing-medicaid-medicare-chip-and-aca-assistance-to-essential-workers-1iwd3Ds0Uxm)

**Benefits Data Trust**
- **What it did:** data-driven outreach and applications for Medicare
  Savings Programs, Extra Help and SNAP. Sold to states and health plans
  (e.g. UPMC Health Plan).
- **Status:** **closed in August 2024**, after a $9.8M loss in 2023.
- **Source:** [Inquirer](https://www.inquirer.com/health/benefits-data-trust-closing-august-23-20240823.html)
- **Read:** the outreach model works, but it was grant- and contract-funded.
  Paying per approval out of a fund's own savings is a different engine.
  *(inferred)*

- **What it does:** Medicare guidance, with a $75M Series D in 2025.
- **Partners:** employers, health systems, financial firms.
- **Business model:** paid largely by commissions on individual Medicare

## Adjacent: navigation already sold to union funds

**Included Health and Transcarent**
- **What they do:** care navigation and concierge, sold to Taft-Hartley funds
  (e.g. MO-KAN Sheet Metal Workers Welfare Fund).
- **Why they're relevant:** they're already inside fund budgets, but they
  steer care. They don't move costs to Medicare or the state.
- **Sources:** [Included Health](https://includedhealth.com/?p=32205),
  [Transcarent for labor](https://www.transcarent.com/transcarent-for-labor-unions)

**TPAs** (Zenith American, BeneSys, MagnaCare and others)
- **What they do:** run fund offices: eligibility, COBRA, dependent audits,
  member portals (e.g. Bridgeway's MemberView).
- **Why they matter:** they're the channel to funds, and their data is our
  trigger feed.
- **Sources:** [Zenith](https://www.zenith-american.com/blog/significant-fund-savings-through-zeniths-dependent-audit-verification-process),
  [Bridgeway](https://www.bridgewaybentech.com/news/new-member-portal-082125)

## Claims-side: other coverage and recovery

**CAQH COB Smart, Gainwell (HMS), Optum and subrogation vendors**
- **What they do:** find other coverage in claims and eligibility data, then
  recover after payment.
- **Why it isn't us:** no member action, and a crowded market.
- **Source:** [CAQH COB Smart](https://www.caqh.org/hubfs/43908627/drupal/solutions/cob-smart/cobsmart-overview.pdf)

**What's actually ours:** the member-side line, by text and in Spanish, that
covers every leak for a union fund, and is paid per approval.

**What isn't ours:** being "the SSDI company." Four firms do that, with
decades of awards. For disability we either beat them on reach (a text from
the fund at the moment of the disability pension application) or route those
cases to one of them as a partner.

**Lead with:** the Medicare Savings Program and Extra Help (open), the
Part B double-pay check, and the text questions nobody asks. Disability is
the second product, not the first.
