# Garment/textile, industrial, Teamsters and misc. funds: target research for Mycelium (MSP / Part B savings)

Researched 2026-10-07. All URLs accessed 2026-10-07. Labels: **verified** = primary source (fund website, SPD, fund notice, fund filing with PBGC/DOL); **secondary** = trusted third party (news, union page that isn't the fund, regulator report); **inferred** = our reasoning from the quoted sources or from the Form 5500 extract, not stated by anyone. "Not found" means we searched and found nothing. It does not mean the benefit doesn't exist.

Form 5500 numbers are from `pension_plans.csv` / `health_plans.csv` in this folder (2024 plan year). `avg_monthly_benefit` = benefits paid / recipients / 12, a screening number only. In `health_plans.csv`, `participants_boy` is total participants at the start of the year and `active` is active participants at the end of the year (see `build_list.py`). So "participants minus active" is only a rough proxy for retirees and other non-actives (inferred).

## Summary (plain English)

1. **Only one fund in this group pays back Part B: MILA (longshore health).** It pays the **standard Part B premium**, but only to pensioners who leave MILA's wrap-around plan for a Medicare Advantage plan with drug coverage, and only "when you supply proof of payment". If MSP pays the premium, that payment should stop, which is the saving (inferred). We don't know how many MILA pensioners take this route or how many live in NY.
2. **The National Retirement Fund (NRF) and UNITE HERE Retirement Fund Legacy Plans have a small, closed Part B benefit.** Former Local 15 (NYC hotel) participants with pre-1987 service get "a monthly supplemental pension equal to the monthly premium required for Medicare Part B". As written it is a pension benefit, not a reimbursement tied to paying the premium, so MSP probably would not reduce it (inferred). The group is old and small.
3. **No Part B reimbursement found for any other fund in this group.** Several Teamsters funds clearly stop retiree medical at 65: Local 807, Road Carriers Local 707, and Local 295 ("Retirees under the age of 65"). Local 813's site says "There are no medical benefits after retirement." The CIRS life plan says "Coverage ends upon termination or retirement."
4. **Two funds do cover Medicare-age retirees, but without a Part B payment: Local 812 Health Fund and Sidney Hillman Health Center.** Local 812 pays secondary to Medicare. Hillman (Rochester) offers supplemental clinic, pharmacy and optical benefits and requires retirees to keep primary coverage such as "Medicare part B". Hillman already helps retirees with **EPIC** enrollment.
5. **The biggest low-income pool is NRF Legacy:** 86,306 retirees averaging about $147/mo. Next is the Amalgamated Insurance Fund retirement plan: 15,226 retirees at about $78/mo. Nearly all would pass MSP income tests on the pension alone (inferred, since Social Security and other income are unknown). There is **no fund saving** there, and **we found no NY-vs-other-state split** for any of these national funds.
6. **NRF is the hub.** Amalgamated Employee Benefits Administrators (AEBA, 333 Westchester Ave, White Plains) runs NRF, UNITE HERE RF, the Amalgamated Insurance Fund, Consolidated and the Amalgamated National Health Fund. NRF also owns Amalgamated Life through ALICO Services Corp. (NY DFS, 2017). A single relationship would reach about 135k retirees in the CSV rows alone (inferred), but as a member benefit, not a saving.
7. **"Nurses and Local 813 IBT Retirement Plan" is largely a Massachusetts nurses plan.** The Boston Globe says it pools the legacy Caritas Christi plan with "about 1,800 Teamsters", and its pensions are paid to "about 11,000 beneficiaries in the state" (Massachusetts). **Massachusetts Service Employees' Pension Fund** is a 32BJ fund administered from Boston. Both have a small NY share (inferred).
8. **Most health funds here show almost no non-active participants.** Laundry/Workers United, United Wire, Hollow Metal, United Teamster Fund, Local 210, Local 807 and Local 707 all have participants roughly equal to actives. That points to little or no retiree coverage (inferred). The exceptions are MILA (~11k non-active), Amalgamated National Health Fund (~11.8k), Sidney Hillman (~5k), Local 812 (~1.1k) and Local 713 (~1k).
9. **Named leaders:** MILA Executive Director **LaVerne Thompson** (111 Broadway, NYC). Hollow Metal Pension Fund Executive Director **Denise Ortiz**. CIRS Executive Director **Richard Scheer**. MSEPF Fund Administrator **Peter Goldberger**. No executive director is named publicly for NRF, UNITE HERE RF, ANHF or the Teamsters funds.
10. **Bottom line:** none of these funds is an "A" Part B-savings pilot. **MILA is the only savings pitch (B).** **NRF/AEBA plus Sidney Hillman are the best member-benefit partners (B)**: very low pensions, and Hillman already does EPIC navigation. Everything else is C.

## Findings

### A. MILA National Health Plan (longshore, ILA)

1. **verified: retiree coverage tiers.** MILA "provides medical, behavioral health, prescription drug, dental, and vision benefits to eligible active and retired workers". It has charts for "Pensioners age 62 or older who are not eligible to enroll for Medicare" (Premier), "Pensioners, ages 58 through 61" (Basic), and "Pensioners who are eligible to enroll for Medicare for MILA Medicare Wrap-Around Plan." https://www.iladistrict.com/mila/
2. **verified: Part B enrollment required.** "It is extremely important that each person who is eligible for MILA Medicare Wrap-Around plan benefits enroll in Medicare Parts A and B ... coverage under the MILA Medicare Wrap-Around plan is incomplete without Medicare coverage in both Parts A and B." The Wrap-Around chart says: "The covered person must enroll in Medicare, Part A and Part B." MILA SPD (2021), p. 16. https://www.milamhctf.com/pdfs/MILA-SPD-2021.pdf
3. **verified: Part B reimbursement for Medicare Advantage electors.** "If the Medicare Advantage Plan contains a Medicare Part D prescription drug benefit, MILA will reimburse you for the standard cost of the Medicare Part B premium. This is the premium you must pay unless your income has caused Medicare to charge you a higher premium. It does not contain 'late entrant' penalty charges." (SPD p. 16.) "MILA will reimburse you for the cost of your regular Medicare Part B premium (the standard premium) when you supply proof of payment." (p. 73.) "If you enroll in this type of plan that provides Part D coverage, the only benefit which MILA provides is reimbursement for the standard monthly Medicare Part B premium." (p. 84.) "MILA will reimburse you quarterly following the end of the calendar quarter ... for the standard premium." (p. 85.) Same URL as finding 2.
   - **verified: limits.** "If you elect a Medicare Advantage Plan option without prescription medication coverage and you do not purchase Medicare Part D separately, MILA will not reimburse you for the standard monthly Medicare Part B premium." (p. 85.) The dental chart lists "Retired Members — Medicare Advantage Plan if qualified for Medicare Part B premium reimbursement". (Same URL.)
   - **inferred: the saving.** Once MSP pays the Part B premium, the pensioner has no premium payment to prove, so MILA's quarterly reimbursement (2026: $202.90/mo, $2,434.80/yr per person) should stop. This applies only to the Medicare Advantage + Part D subgroup. Wrap-Around pensioners get no Part B payment, so MSP saves MILA nothing for them.
4. **verified: Port Association retiree rule** (one example of eligibility): "at age 65, or upon becoming eligible to enroll in Medicare, if earlier, the Port Association Retiree will receive MILA Medicare Wrap-Around benefits, provided that the Port Association Retiree has enrolled in Medicare Parts A and B." (SPD p. 83.)
5. **verified: geography.** The SPD refers to "longshore employees working at ports on the Atlantic and Gulf" coasts and to "ports in the United States, except Alaska and Hawaii". (Same URL.) **NY share: not found.**
6. **verified: office and leader.** "LaVerne Thompson, Executive Director, MILA Managed Health Care Trust Fund, 111 Broadway, Suite 502, New York, NY 10006-1901, Telephone: 212-766-5700". The Plan Administrator is "MILA Managed Health Care Trust Fund". (Same URL, SPD front matter and p. ~110.)
7. **inferred (5500 extract):** MILA had 34,925 participants and 23,890 active, so about 11,000 non-active (pensioners and others). The MILA pension plan isn't in our NY pension extract, so we don't know retiree pension levels.
8. **Caveat:** the SPD linked from the ILA site is dated 2021. We found no later SMM changing the Part B rule, but did not search MILA's own site exhaustively.

## Conflicts and inconsistencies

- **UNITE HERE RF size:** the site says "more than 62,600" members, the CSV shows 89,339 Legacy participants, and PLANSPONSOR says 91,744. Different dates and definitions are likely (inferred).
- **Nurses and Local 813:** the Globe's "about 11,000 beneficiaries in the state" vs. 6,338 retirees + 583 beneficiaries in the CSV. The Globe figure may include deferred vested participants or the whole plan (inferred).
- **Local 15 supplement:** it appears in both the NRF SPD (2011) and the UNITE HERE RF SPD (2018), and it is unclear which plan pays it today.
