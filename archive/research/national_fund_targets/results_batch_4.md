# National fund targets — batch 4 results (30 plans)

Accessed 2026-10-07. Sources are the funds' own documents unless the label says otherwise.
"verified" means read in the fund's own document or website. "secondary" means another source,
such as a union local's site. "inferred" means a conclusion drawn from what a fund document says
or leaves out.

**Coverage caveat:** The shared web-search budget for this session ran out partway through the
batch. The remaining plans were checked only through fund websites already found or reachable
directly, so **16 of 30 plans are `unknown`** on Part B. These are listed at the end and should be
re-run.

## Summary

- **Part B premium reimbursement confirmed: 1 of 30, MILA National Health Plan (NY).** MILA has
  two Part B programs:
  1. Pensioners who leave the MILA Medicare Wrap-Around Plan for a Medicare Advantage plan get the
     **standard Part B premium reimbursed**.
  2. A Retiree-Only Medicare Part B Premium Payment Reimbursement Plan, for retirees with retirement
     dates on or after 10/1/2023, reimburses the **full Part B premium, including IRMAA**, for the
     retiree and spouse. It pays for up to **24 months** after retirement.

  MILA is the strongest target in this batch. It has 11,035 non-active participants.
- **HRA that can pay Part B: 4 plans (weak signal).**
  - **Hanford Retiree Welfare Benefit Plan (WA):** a sponsor-funded **$1,800 per year** HRA for
    Medicare-eligible retirees who buy coverage through Extend Health/Via Benefits. Insurance
    premiums are an eligible expense. This is a DOE-contractor plan, not a Taft-Hartley fund.
  - **Mid Central Operating Engineers (IN), Operating Engineers Local 139 (WI) and Operating
    Engineers #49 (MN):** each has an individual HRA built from employer contributions while
    working. Mid Central's SPD names "Medicare Part B premiums" as an eligible expense; Local 139's
    names "Medicare" premiums. Local 49's HRA lists retiree premiums. The fund's own money does not
    pay Part B.
- **Part B premium explicitly not reimbursed, or none found in the fund's retiree documents: 9
  plans.**
  - **UEBT Retiree Health Plan (CA)** paid Part B for some retirees until it ended the benefit on
    3/1/2013 ("Medicare Part B premiums will no longer be reimbursed for any retirees").
  - **Carpenters H&W Trust Fund for California** says retirees "must pay for Part B."
  - **Motion Picture Industry Health Plan, Directors Guild of America - Producer Health Plan, UMWA
    Combined Benefit Fund, National Automatic Sprinkler Industry Welfare Fund, Division 1181 ATU,
    Joint Industry Board / Electrical Industry plan and 1199SEIU Greater New York:** Medicare-age
    retirees are covered, but no Part B premium reimbursement appears in the documents read.
  - The **sister 1199SEIU National Benefit Fund** (a different EIN) does reimburse 50% of the
    standard Part B premium. This needs a check of whether Greater New York retirees can get the
    same.
- **Covers Medicare-age (post-65) retirees: 14 of 30 confirmed**, plus IBEW Local 102 from the
  union's site. The rest are unknown.
- **Notable non-Part-B help:** the Joint Industry Board plan reimburses the Part B *deductible*.
  The 1199 Greater New York fund may reimburse the *Part D* premium for retirees outside the NYC
  area. No plan in this batch publicly mentions Medicare Savings Program, Extra Help or Medicaid
  enrollment help. The Carpenters CA SPD notes the Part B premium must be paid "if not otherwise
  paid for under Medicaid."
