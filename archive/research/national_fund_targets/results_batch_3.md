# National fund targets — batch 3 results (30 plans)

Accessed 2026-10-07. Source list: `batch_3.csv` (DOL Form 5500; `non_active` = rough retiree proxy).

**Coverage caveat:** the shared web-search budget for this session ran out after about half the plans had been searched. From then on, the only remaining sources were fund URLs already found (fetched directly, or from web.archive.org when blocked). Plans marked `unknown` with "search budget exhausted" were **not** searched the full ~5 times. They should be re-run, not read as "no benefit".

## Summary

- **Funds confirmed to reimburse Part B: 2 of 30.**
  - **ILWU-PMA Welfare Plan** (CA/OR/WA, 9,268 non-active) pays the **full** premium. Source: the fund's own site: "The Welfare Plan reimburses the cost of Medicare Part B premiums ... to retired employees, survivors and dependents who are required to enroll in Medicare Part B." **Best target in this batch.**
  - **1199SEIU National Benefit Fund** (NY, 39,673 non-active) pays **50% of the standard Part B premium for retiree and spouse**. This is on its own claim form. The fund's NBF retiree page sets eligibility at age 65+ with 10 years of service. **Largest target in this batch.**
- **HRA or allowance a retiree could use for Part B: 3 plans.** These are weaker, because the fund saves money only if the account or allowance is actually spent on Part B.
  - **Midwest Operating Engineers Retiree Welfare Plan**: the Retiree Medical Savings Plan account can pay Medicare premiums.
  - **Plumbers' Welfare Fund Local 130 Retiree Medical Plan**: the retiree HRA lists "Medicare premiums".
  - **Laborers H&W Trust for Northern California**: a $75/month Health Care Expense Assistance Benefit (2016 rules). It is not earmarked for Part B.
  - A fourth, the **Northwest Carpenters** retiree plan, is weaker still: its archived 2013 SPD lists a retiree HRA. Whether that HRA covers Medicare premiums was not confirmed.
- **Confirmed post-65 retiree coverage: about 10 plans.** These are 1199SEIU NBF, ILWU-PMA, Midwest OE RWP, Teamsters Western Region & NJ (Local 177), UMWA 1992 Plan, NorCal Laborers, Plumbers 130 Retiree, LA County Fire Fighters Local 1014 (secondary source) and Northwest Carpenters (partial). The GMP-Employers Retiree Trust is a retiree-only trust, but how it treats Medicare-age retirees was not confirmed. Most of these coordinate with Medicare, offer a Medicare Advantage plan, or charge a retiree self-pay premium **without** reimbursing Part B.
- **Not useful as a target:**
  - Teamsters Local 639 (this row is the *pension* fund, plan 001, not a health plan).
  - NFL Non-Vested Former Player Wellness Plan (a counseling benefit).
  - NFL Player Insurance Plan: it gives 5 years of Continuing Veteran coverage only. The NFL's $160–$200/month Medicare subsidy is paid by a different plan, the Former Player Life Improvement Plan.
- **No plan in this batch showed existing MSP, Extra Help or Medicaid enrollment help.**
- **Unresolved: 15 plans** (mostly because the search budget ran out). They include large funds worth re-checking: Teamsters & Food Employers (CA), IAFF MERP (WA, a retiree reimbursement plan that could plausibly pay Medicare premiums), Steamfitters Industry Welfare Fund (NY), Local 103 IBEW (MA), IUOE Local 94 (NY), MM&P (MD) and the Philadelphia hospital fund (PA).

## Findings (one per plan)

1. **1199SEIU National Benefit Fund (NY, EIN 131628401).**
   - Fund office: 1199SEIU Benefit Funds, PO Box 2661, New York, NY 10108-2661, (646) 473-8666.
   - Claim form (01/24): "Eligible retirees may submit a claim for 50 percent of the standard Medicare Part B premium for the retiree and spouse. Eligibility is based on years of service and age at retirement. Check your Summary Plan Description for details."
   - Also on the form: "Claims may be filed once every quarter, but no later than two years after the premium payment."
   - NBF retiree page (https://1199seiubenefits.org/hce-pension/retiree-health-benefits): the 65+ row with 10 years of service "Includes Rx, vision, hearing, dental, life insurance, burial and 50% Part B premium reimbursement".
   - The Greater New York fund (a separate fund) has different rules: 25 years of service and an Aetna Medicare Advantage plan.
   - Label: verified. No MSP or Extra Help help found.
   - **MSP impact:** each MSP enrollee saves the fund about $101.45/month (50% × $202.90), plus the same for an enrolled spouse.
2. **Northwest Carpenters Health and Security Plan (EIN 916029049; filed as CA, but the trust is Carpenters Trusts of Western Washington / Northwest Carpenters Trusts, administered by Northwest Administrators).**
   - A search snippet of the fund's retiree SPD page (ctww.org/html/hsspd-001320.htm, now offline) says the Retired Carpenters plan's "monthly contribution rates are also established by the Board of Trustees and are directly related to ... each individual's eligibility for Medicare". It also mentions a service-based subsidy.
   - The archived 2013 SPD index includes a "Retiree Health Reimbursement Accounts" section and a "Carpenters Health and Security Plan—For Retired Carpenters" section. Their text could not be retrieved.
   - No Part B reimbursement was found.
   - Label: secondary/inferred. **Needs a primary SPD.**
3. **G.M.P.-Employers Retiree Trust (Fort Myers, FL, EIN 236411794).**
   - The USW page says the Trust is "created in collective bargaining negotiations to provide post-retirement health care coverage to eligible retirees and their spouses".
   - Search summary of that page: retirees pay nothing for the Indemnity option, and spouses pay $35/month. A direct fetch of the page returned only navigation, so this is unverified.
   - No Medicare or Part B detail was found.
   - Label: secondary. Retiree health: unknown (probably post65, but unconfirmed). Part B: unknown.
4. **NFL Non-Vested Former Player Wellness Benefit Plan (NY, EIN 133077470 PN 503).**
   - Per an NFL benefits summary quoted on secondary sites, the plan "provides counseling and other support services for players not vested in the Retirement Plan as well as their household members and dependents."
   - This is not retiree medical coverage. Part B: none. Label: secondary. Not a target.
5. **ILWU-PMA Welfare Plan (San Francisco, CA, EIN 946068578).**
   - Administrator: ILWU-PMA Benefit Plans, (415) 673-8500, benefitplans.org.
   - Fund site, Medicare page: "The Welfare Plan reimburses the cost of Medicare Part B premiums, unless otherwise reimbursed, to retired employees, survivors and dependents who are required to enroll in Medicare Part B in order to maintain Welfare Plan coverage."
   - Corroborating union sources (secondary):
     - ILWU Local 13 bulletin #41-15 (Jan/Feb 2015): "If retired and your Medicare premiums increase, please submit your SSA statement to ILWU/PMA Benefit Plans ... to receive adjustment on your benefit."
     - March 2026 bulletin: retirees "will be required to enroll in Medicare Part A and Part B to maintain your retiree health coverage."
   - Label: verified. Part B: **full**, including survivors and dependents. No MSP help found.
6. **Midwest Operating Engineers Retiree Welfare Plan (Countryside, IL; IUOE Local 150; EIN 371752023).**
   - Member services: 708-579-6600.
   - The plan is secondary to Medicare. The 2026 post-Medicare schedule says retirees "must enroll in both Medicare Part A and Part B".
   - SPD: "You must make self-payments for Retiree Welfare Plan benefits ... your RMSP account can be used to pay for your and your eligible dependent's Medicare premiums".
   - Label: verified. Part B: hra (the retiree's own Retiree Medical Savings Plan balance).
   - **MSP impact:** the fund does not pay Part B directly, so MSP would mainly save the retiree's RMSP balance.

13. **Laborers Health and Welfare Trust Fund for Northern California (CA, EIN 941235152).**
    - Administrator: Laborers Funds Administrative Office, 800-244-4530.
    - Retired Plan rules (3/2016), Health Care Expense Assistance Benefit:
      - "$75.00 per month for an eligible pensioner; or $37.50 per month for an eligible surviving spouse beneficiary."
      - It goes to "Senior" pensioners (age 65+), or Medicare-eligible disabled pensioners, who had 2,000 hours in the 48 months before retirement and are in good standing.
      - People who receive it are not eligible for the Fund's medical benefits unless they self-pay.
    - For Fund medical coverage, the Fund pays "the amount of the Medicare Part B Deductible plus any copayment or coinsurance", which is cost-sharing, not the premium.
    - 2026 comparison: retirees pay the full rate unless eligible for a 25% or 50% subsidy (for example, 50% at age 55+ with 25 years of service, or at age 70).
    - Label: verified. Part B: hra-type flat allowance, not earmarked for Part B. Whether HCEA is still current in 2026 was not confirmed.
