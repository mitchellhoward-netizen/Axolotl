# National fund targets: second pass 1 results (20 plans)

Every source below was accessed on 2026-10-07.

Labels:
- **verified**: read in the fund's own website or plan document.
- **secondary**: another source, such as a union page, carrier document, nonprofit database or search-result summary.
- **inferred**: our own reading where no source says it outright.

Two plans were not re-searched. 32BJ and the NYC District Council of Carpenters are carried over from `archive/research/ny_fund_targets/funds_service.md` and `funds_trades.md`.

**Search caveat.** About 44 web searches were used across the 18 remaining plans. The search engine mostly returned ProPublica or GuideStar profiles, or results about unrelated public-sector plans. Some fund sites could not be read:
- **Blocked:** the WPAS (Seattle) fund site for Locals 302 & 612 is behind Cloudflare (403), and nwadmin.com returns 403.
- **Login required:** the CBT Funds, MLB Players and Ohio Laborers sites have no public plan pages, or the domain could not be found.
- **No program text:** ProPublica's 990 XML and PDF files sit behind a bot check, so program descriptions could not be read.

## Summary

- **No plan in this batch is confirmed to pay or reimburse the Part B premium directly** (full, partial or flat).
- **3 plans have an HRA that can pay Part B premiums.** In each case the money is the member's own hours-based balance, not a fund subsidy. MSP would protect the member's balance more than it would cut fund cost (inferred).
  - **Pipe Fitters Local 597 (IL), the strongest of the three.** HRA money is automatically reimbursed for "Medicare premium payments on record with the Fund Office". It also has a post-65 Humana group MAPD (2026) and an older Medicare supplemental plan (2021 SPD). Verified.
  - **Eastern Atlantic States Carpenters (PA/NJ).** The HRA lists "Medicare Part B and Medicare Part D premiums" as eligible expenses. Medicare retirees are auto-enrolled in Blue Medicare Advantage and pay a premium. Verified.
  - **North Atlantic States Carpenters (MA), New York region only.** The NY HRA SPD lists "Medicare" under eligible medical premiums, and Medicare-eligible retirees may use their remaining HRA balance. The SPD is dated July 2017. The New England retiree plan ends at 65. Verified.
- **3 plans have post-65 coverage, but the retiree pays Part B:**
  - **SoCal UFCW & Food Employers Benefit Fund (Cypress, CA):** Medicare retirees pay $101.50/mo single from 4/1/2026, and must enroll in Parts A and B. Verified.
  - **NYC Carpenters:** retirees get UHC Medicare Advantage, and the retiree must "pay the Medicare Part B premium". Verified, from the earlier NY pass.
  - **SMART-TD:** a GA-23111 Plan F Medicare supplement is listed for retired TD (UTU) members, with no Part B payment found. Secondary.
- **2 plans cover retirees only until 65 or Medicare:**
  - **North Atlantic States Carpenters (New England):** retiree coverage runs up to 36 months, for retirees "under age 65 and not otherwise eligible for Medicare". Verified.
  - **32BJ:** carried over from the earlier NY pass. Verified.
- **12 plans are still unknown on Part B:**
  - Amalgamated National Health Fund
  - MLB Players Welfare Plan
  - Retirees Welfare Trust (WA)
  - Tri-State Joint Fund (CT; GuideStar says it covers "active and retired members")
  - Teamsters & Food Employers Security Trust (CA)
  - SF Culinary
  - California Ironworkers Field Welfare Plan
  - Ohio Operating Engineers
  - OLDC-Ohio Contractors Association (one secondary source mentions "retiree insurance")
  - Cleveland Bakers & Teamsters (its website lists Humana as a vendor, not explained)
  - Locals 302 & 612
  - Sheet Metal 104
- **Minnesota Laborers** covers retirees in some form (verified), but no Medicare detail was found.
- **Best leads for Mycelium:**
  1. **Local 597** and **EAS Carpenters.** In both, retirees can spend their HRA on Part B, so MSP would leave members more HRA money for other costs. They also run group Medicare plans, so the funds may be open to member-benefit outreach.
  2. **Retirees Welfare Trust (WA).** It is a retiree-only VEBA with $48M of program revenue (FY2024). It is worth a phone call to Northwest Administrators, 2323 Eastlake Ave E, Seattle.

## Findings

1. **SMART-TD Health & Welfare Plan (VA, 80-0616629). Secondary.**
   - Administrator: claims go to UnitedHealthcare (Plan F) and Highmark (NRC/UTU plan 690100). The sponsor/administrator for this EIN was not confirmed.
   - The SMART-TD retiree benefits directory (10/2022) says: "For retired SMART-TD (UTU) members and others covered under GA-23111 Plan F, the Medicare Supplement, send claims to UnitedHealthcare".
   - The only Part B mention is: "Retired employees and/or dependents entitled to Railroad Medicare who need information about their Part B benefits may contact Palmetto GBA."
   - The directory names the "NRC/UTU Health and Welfare Plan (690100)". It never uses the name "SMART-TD Health & Welfare Plan", so which rail plan this EIN belongs to is not settled.
   - URL: https://static.smart-union.org/worksite/PDFs/RetireeNews/H%26W+Directory+of+Benefits_1022.pdf
2. **UFCW Unions & Food Employers Benefit Fund (CA, 95-2301788). Verified for the fund; the EIN match is inferred.**
   - Administrator: Southern California United Food & Commercial Workers Unions and Food Employers Joint Benefit Funds Administration, LLC.
   - Why we think this is the right fund: ProPublica lists the EIN in Cypress, CA, the same city as the SoCal UFCW fund office.
   - The Medicare enrollment page says: "You are required to enroll in both Medicare Part A and Part B as soon as you are eligible."
   - The retiree plan page says: "For retirees and their dependents who are Medicare-eligible, Medicare serves as their primary coverage, and this Fund's coverage is secondary."
   - The monthly costs page says: "Starting April 1, 2026, the monthly retiree premium contribution rates to health care will be: Single: If you're eligible for Medicare: $101.50".
   - The fund's HRA is an active-participant wellness HRA. No Part B reimbursement was found.
   - URLs: https://www.scufcwfunds.com/eligibility-enrollment/retirees/enrollment/medicare-enrollment/ , https://www.scufcwfunds.com/healthcare/retirees/retirees-except-class-e/

   - Administrator: EAS Carpenters Benefit Funds (Philadelphia; Fund Office 215-568-0430).

   - The Blue MA page says retirees must enroll in Parts A and B, and that "you will be automatically enrolled in this plan."
   - The HRA page lists eligible expenses as "Premiums you pay for insurance that covers the expenses of medical care … including Medicare Part B and Medicare Part D premiums".
   - The HRA is "an employer-paid contribution based on hours worked". Whether retirees can draw down their balance after retiring is not stated on the page (inferred yes, since it is described as covering "gaps in your health coverage, including premium").
   - URLs: https://members.carpenters.fund/benefit-coverage/retiree-health-coverage/ , https://members.carpenters.fund/benefit-coverage/blue-medicare-advantage/ , https://members.carpenters.fund/benefit-coverage/health-reimbursement-account-hra/
