SUBJECT: OBSOLESCENCE OF GUIDANCE RELATED TO ASSET ELIMINATION

This letter supersedes the policy guidance in All County Welfare Director Letters
(ACWDLs) 23-20, 23-21, 23-22, and 23-22E, which provided counties with guidance
regarding determining Non-MAGI program eligibility starting January 1, 2024,
without considering the treatment or value of the specified asset types.

Due to Assembly Bill 116 (Chapter 21, Statutes of 2025), the asset test will be
reinstated for Non-MAGI programs, including LTC and MSPs, with the exception of
the Pickle, DAC, and DW programs, effective January 1, 2026. As of January 1, 2026,
ACWDL 23-20, 23-21, 23-22, and 23-22E are obsolete.

Counties shall utilize the policy guidance and regulations below when determining
Non-MAGI eligibility starting January 1, 2026:

• Annuities: Title 22, California Code of Regulations (CCR) § 50507(a)(2),
50489.5(g), Medi-Cal Eligibility Procedures Manual (MEPM) Article 9J.
• IRAs, Retirement Plans for Self-Employed Individuals, Work-Related
Pension Plans: ACWDL 02-51, ACWDL 90-01 Sections 50402 & 50458.
• Rental Property: 22 CCR § 50503(a)(8), 50507(a)(18), and 50508.
• Interest and Dividends: 22 CCR § 50507(a)(19)-(20), ACWDL 92-25, ACWDL
94-73, ACWDL 96-48, ACWDL 99-31, ACWDL 05-17; ACWDL 09-54, ACWDL
12-07
• Trusts: 22 CCR § 50489, 50489.5, and 50489.9, Medi-Cal Eligibility Procedures
Manual Article 9J and Article 10
