SUBJECT: IMPLEMENTATION OF NEW STATE ONLY AID CODES FOR INDIVIDUALS
WHO ARE NOT ELIGIBLE FOR FEDERALLY FUNDED FULL SCOPE MEDI-CAL

The purpose of this Medi-Cal Eligibility Division Information Letter (MEDIL) is to inform
counties of fifteen aid codes, including nine new aid codes and six repurposed aid
codes, which will be used to identify adult individuals aged 19-64 who are not eligible
for federally funded full scope Medi-Cal.

Background
Current federal law states that federally funded full scope Medicaid is available to
immigrants who are Qualified Non-Citizens (QNC). QNCs include immigrants (also
known as “qualified aliens”) who have been in the United States for a minimum of five
years, those who are exempt from the five-year waiting period (also known as the five-
year bar), and lawfully present immigrants who are under 21 years of age and/or
pregnant. The five-year bar restricts certain QNCs’ access to federal public benefit
programs for the first five years from the date they obtain their qualifying immigration
status. These individuals are a subset of QNCs and are referred to as Newly Qualified
Immigrants (NQI). Federal Financial Participation (FFP) is limited to emergency and
pregnancy-related services for individuals who do not have an immigration status or are
unable to establish an immigration status eligible for federally funded full scope
Medicaid (known as Medi-Cal in California).

For many immigrants who are ineligible for federally funded full scope Medi-Cal,
California uses state funds to provide full scope Medi-Cal. If an individual is not eligible
for federally funded or state funded full scope Medi-Cal, they may still qualify for
restricted scope Medi-Cal, which covers emergency, pregnancy-related services, and
long-term care.

Implementation Date
The implementation date for the new aid codes is January 1, 2027. Individuals who apply
on or after January 1, 2027, must be placed in the new aid codes, as indicated. Existing
members will be transitioned to the new aid codes beginning with the January 2027
month of eligibility. Additional information regarding the group that will be transitioned
to the new aid codes will be communicated in forthcoming guidance.

Aid Code Description
The Department of Health Care Services (DHCS) is implementing fifteen aid codes,
including nine new aid codes (W9, V4, Y1, V5, Y2, V6, W5, W6, 0K) and six existing aid
codes (1C, 1D, 1J, 1K, 1M, 1N). The aid codes identify individuals who are not eligible for
federally funded full scope Medi-Cal based on immigration status groupings, share of
cost, and funding. These aid codes will provide the same level of benefits as full scope
Medi-Cal coverage. FFP must be claimed only for Medi-Cal covered emergency and
pregnancy services. All Medi-Cal covered services provided by these aid codes that are
not emergency or pregnancy services must be paid for with 100 percent State General
Fund. The 15 aid codes are defined below and included in the table in Enclosure 1.
