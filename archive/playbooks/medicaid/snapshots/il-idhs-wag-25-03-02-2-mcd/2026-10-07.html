MAGI Deduction Limits

The MAGI deductions listed below have an annual cap.  To see all the MAGI deductions, go to PM 08-03-03.

MAGI Deductions that are Limited For Tax Year 2025
Health Savings Account Contribution Limit
IRS Form 8889		 Single person:  $4,300 per tax year
Family:  $8,550 per tax year

Moving Expense Limit

Form 3903

 The new workplace must be 50 miles or more further from the person's old home. Moving Expense is no longer an allowable deduction beginning with tax year 2018, except for active duty members of the Armed Forces who are ordered to move or change duty stations.

Student Loan Interest Limit

1098-E

 $2,500 in a tax year. Certain rules apply, see PM 08-03-03.
IRA Deduction

Age: less than 50: $7,000 per year

 50 and older: $8,000 per year
Educator Expense

Single Person: Revised. $300 per year

Family: Revised. $600 per year

Tuition and Fees Limit

Form 1098-T

 Tuition and Fees paid are no longer deductible in calculating MAGI, effective January 1, 2018.
