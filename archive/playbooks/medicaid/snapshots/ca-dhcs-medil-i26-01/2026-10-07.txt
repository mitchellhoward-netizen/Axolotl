SUBJECT: AVAILABILITY OF THE UPDATED SINGLE STREAMLINED
APPLICATION (REFERENCE: MEDI-CAL ELIGIBILITY INFORMATION
LETTER I 25-24)

The updated Single Streamlined Application (SSApp) is now available in English on the
Department of Health Care Services (DHCS) website for printing and mailing to local
county offices. Counties and applicants can access the form
at: https://www.dhcs.ca.gov/Medi-Cal/Pages/apply.aspx. DHCS continues to print
the SSApp packet only in English and Spanish. The updated English SSApp packet is
available for counties and community partners to order using the MC 0026 form. The
updated Spanish-language packet will be available on the DHCS website and for
ordering later in 2026.

Additionally, the updated SSApp will be available in threshold languages and large print
in mail-in format on the DHCS website in 2026. In the meantime, counties may continue
using the existing English and Spanish paper SSApp versions in their inventory, as well
as the threshold-language applications available on the DHCS website.

DHCS will provide future updates on the availability of the SSApp in Spanish and the
other threshold languages.

Refer to MEDIL 25-24 for details regarding changes in the updated SSApp.

If you have any questions or for further information, please contact MCED-
Policy@dhcs.ca.gov.
