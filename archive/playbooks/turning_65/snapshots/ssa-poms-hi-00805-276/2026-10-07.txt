HI 00805.276 SMI Effective Date for SEP Enrollments - Group Health Plans

Policy

If an individual enrolls in SMI or Premium-HI while still enrolled in a GHP/LGHP or
during the first full month when not enrolled in a GHP/LGHP based on current employment
status, coverage begins with either:

•

the first day of the month of SMI or Premium-HI enrollment, or

•

at the individual's option, with the first day of any of the following three months.

If the individual enrolls in SMI or Premium-HI during any of the remaining 7 months
of the special enrollment period, coverage begins with the first day of the month
after the month of enrollment.
