Benefit Access Program

The Benefit Access Program offers two benefits for seniors and persons with disabilities.

Transit Benefit


Seniors Ride Free 
Transit Benefit

Persons with Disabilities Ride Free 
Secretary of State


License Plate Discount

Qualifications

Age Requirements:

  • 65 years of age or older before January 1 of the current year; 
  • You must become 65 years of age this year; or
  • You must be 16 years of age or older before January 1 of the current year and qualify as disabled. 

Residency Requirements:

  • You must live in Illinois at the time you file your application.

Disability Requirements:

  • To qualify as disabled, you must be at least 16 years of age prior to January 1 of the current year and be determined totally and permanently disabled (as defined by program requirements) prior to January 1 of the current year.

Income Requirements:

Your total gross income for the last closed tax year must be less than:

  • $33,562 for a 1 person household (yourself only); or
  • $44,533 for a 2 person household (yourself and your spouse, or yourself and one Qualified Additional Resident); or
  • $55,500 for a 3 person household (yourself, your spouse and one Qualified Additional Resident, or yourself and two Qualified Additional Residents).

You must include your spouse’s income if married and living together on December 31 of last year. If your spouse died last year, you would file as single and claim only your income. Do NOT include the income of a Qualified Additional Resident.

To be determined eligible for these benefits, you must submit a Benefit Access Application on the internet. Paper applications are not available.

Application Guidance & Tools

Expand All Collapse All

  • Once your application is approved, you can print a certificate of eligibility.
  • Take the certificate to:
    • Your local transit authority, or
    • The Secretary of State's Office.
  • The license plate discount will be available 10 business days after approval.
  • If you requested the Ride Free Benefit:
    • Contact your local public transit system for details.
    • Note: Your local transit system may have additional requirements for free rides.

  • You can check your application status on the website 48 hours after submission.

  • Processing times to determine eligibility may take up to 8 weeks, depending on documentation.
  • Please wait 10 business days after approval for your license plate discount to be active.
