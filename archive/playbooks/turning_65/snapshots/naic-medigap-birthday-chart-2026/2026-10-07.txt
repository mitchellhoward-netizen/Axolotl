Alaska NO NO Although Alaska does not have an under 65 Medicare Supplement
coverage requirement, there are insurers that offer <65 coverage,
and the state high risk pool - Alaska Comprehensive Health
Insurance Association (ACHIA) - offers a Medicare carveout plan for
those under age 65 and covered by Medicare.
Many Medicare Supplement companies in Alaska do not have
enough members to have credible Alaska data. These insurers rely
on nationwide experience in their rate filings. Consequently, even
though AK does not have a Birthday Rule or required < 65 coverage,
Alaska believes rating impacts related to these rules may also
impact Alaska’s market.
