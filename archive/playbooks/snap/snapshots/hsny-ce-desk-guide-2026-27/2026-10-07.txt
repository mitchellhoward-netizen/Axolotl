Determining a Household’s Categorical Eligibility for SNAP
Any household with a member who is currently disqualified from SNAP due to an Intentional
Program Violation (IPV) or sanction is not categorically eligible for SNAP. These households
may still qualify for SNAP under regular SNAP budgeting rules.
