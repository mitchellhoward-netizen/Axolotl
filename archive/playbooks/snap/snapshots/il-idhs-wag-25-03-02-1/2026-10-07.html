SNAP Resource Limits

  • $4,750 if at least 1 household member is a Qualifying Member; or
  • $3,000 for all other SNAP households without a Qualifying Member; or
  • $4,750 for lottery or gambling winnings, see PM 07-04-21

Note: SNAP asset limits do not apply to Categorically Eligible households unless the SNAP household receives substantial lottery or gambling winnings of more than $4,750, see PM 07-04-21.

For SNAP Assets, see PM 07-04-00.
