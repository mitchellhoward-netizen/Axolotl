MR# 26.26: October 2026 SNAP Adjustments due to COLA

10/01/2026

Summary

  • This Manual Release includes changes to the Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP) Utility Standards, Maximum Monthly Gross/Net Income Standards and Deductions, the Maximum Excess Shelter Deduction, the Homeless Shelter Standard, the SNAP Asset Limits, and the SNAP Minimum/Maximum Monthly Benefit Amounts. These changes are effective with October 2026 issuances.
  • As a result of the annual review of the State Utility Standards, the Food & Nutrition Administration (FNA) approved changes to the following standards, effective with October 2026 issuances:
    • Air Conditioning/Heating Standard $565
    • Limited Utility Standard $472
    • Single Utility Standard $80
    • Telephone Standard $69
  • For Change Reporting households, the income reporting requirement has increased to $150.00
  • IES system changes will reflect in the SNAP calculations starting with October 2026 issuances.
  • System changes to the Application for Benefits Eligibility (ABE) site have been made to reflect these changes.
  • Updates to the SNAP calculator on the Illinois Department of Human Services (IDHS) site are being made to reflect these changes.
  • The Program Standards Chart for WAG 25-03-02 (1) SNAP has been revised with updated standards.
  • The SNAP Brochure and the Expedited SNAP Screening Form are being revised with the new SNAP standards.
  • The Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP) Change Report Form is being revised with updated amounts.
  • The Change in Reporting Requirements is being revised with updated amounts.
  • The Basis of Issuance (BOI) Table has been revised to reflect the updated SNAP benefit amounts.

SNAP Utility Standards

As a result of the annual review of the State utility standards, FNA approved changes to the SNAP utility standards as follows:

  • Air Conditioning/Heating Standard $565
  • Limited Utility Standard $472
  • Single Utility Standard $80
  • Telephone Standard $69

SNAP Standard

  • Federal law provides for an increase to the Maximum Monthly Gross (165% FPL) and Net Income Standards (100% FPL) effective with October 2026 issuances.
  • The SNAP 200% FPL Gross Income Standards have increased and are used to determine categorical eligibility for qualifying member households.
  • The Standard Deduction amount by SNAP household size is as follows:
    • 1 through 3 persons $213
    • 4 persons $225
    • 5 persons $264
    • 6 or more persons $304
  • The Maximum Excess Shelter Deduction has increased to $769.
  • The Homeless Shelter Standard has increased to $206.
  • The Asset Limits for SNAP are as follows:
    • $4,750 for households with at least 1 qualifying member.
    • $3,000 for all other SNAP households without a qualifying member.
    • $4,750 for Lottery or Gambling winnings.
  • Maximum Monthly SNAP Allotments have increased for all household sizes.
  • The minimum monthly benefit amount for 1-2 person households has increased to $25.
  • The Income Reporting Requirement increases to $150
  • These changes are effective with October 2026 issuances and are reflected in the tables below.
  • The Program Standards Chart for WAG 25-03-02 (1) SNAP has been revised with the new SNAP standards.

System Changes

  • IES system changes will reflect in the SNAP calculations starting with October 2026 issuances.
  • Active SNAP cases will be centrally updated for October 2026 issuances.
  • System changes to ABE/IES have been made to reflect these changes.
  • The SNAP calculator on the IDHS site is currently being updated to reflect these changes.
