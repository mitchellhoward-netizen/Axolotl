SNAP Interview Instructions

Once you've submitted your application or recertification for SNAP, you must have an interview. Please call On Demand at 929-273-1872, Monday - Friday, 8:30 AM to 5:00 PM to complete your interview by phone. Remember to upload your documents using the ACCESS HRA app before calling for your interview. You may get benefits faster by completing your interview sooner. You also have the right to an in-center interview – including a face-to-face interview - at a SNAP Center, regardless of how you submitted your application or recertification form. We cannot turn you away. Find your closest SNAP Center here.
