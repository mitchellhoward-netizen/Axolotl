PM 06-13-02-b: Dual Eligibility (SLIB/Medicaid)

WAG 06-13-02-b

Medicare Part A beneficiaries may be eligible for SLIB if they:

are in spenddown, or

live in a long term care or sheltered care facility.

Community spenddown cases that contain a SLIB are not centrally canceled after the 12-month enrollment period. REDE these cases every 12 months.
