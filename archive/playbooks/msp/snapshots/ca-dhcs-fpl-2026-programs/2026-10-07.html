Program Descriptions by FPL Enclosure 3

Notes: “=” means: eligible if budget unit income is equal to or less than income limit “<” means: eligible if budget unit income is less than income limit “>” means: eligible if budget unit income is greater than income limit

MSP includes Qualified Medicare Beneficiary (QMB), Specified Low-Income Beneficiary (SLMB) and Qualified Individual (QI-1) Programs. For applicants and recipients of the Medicare Savings Programs (MSPs) not receiving RSDI Title II Income, the FPL figures are effective January 1, 2026. For applicants and recipients of MSPs receiving RSDI Title II income, the new FPL figures are effective March 1, 2026.
