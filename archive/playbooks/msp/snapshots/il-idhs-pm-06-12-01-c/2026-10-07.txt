PM 06: Special Programs

PM 06-12-00: Qualified Medicare Beneficiary (QMB) Program

PM 06-12-01: QMB Eligibility

PM 06-12-01-c: Income Exemption

WAG 06-12-01-c

The first $25 of earned or unearned income, other than SSI income, is exempt when determining eligibility for QMB. If the total amount of earned income or unearned income is less than $25, exempt the lesser amount. A client is eligible for no more than one income exemption for their own income, regardless of the types of sources of earned or unearned income.

Allow the income exemption for a spouse or dependent child(ren)'s income if the spouse or dependent child(ren) of a person applying for or receiving MANG is included in the MANG Income Standard and is an applicant or client in another MANG case.
