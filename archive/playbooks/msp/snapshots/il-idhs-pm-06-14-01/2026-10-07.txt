PM 06-14-00: Qualified Individuals Program

PM 06-14-01: QI-1 Eligibility

WAG 06-14-01

To be eligible for QI-1 a person must:

be a current Medicare Part A beneficiary;

meet nonfinancial eligibility requirements:

Social Security Number,

citizenship,

residency, and

assignment of medical support rights;

have income at least 120% but less than 135% of the Federal Poverty Level; and

have nonexempt resources less than the MSP resource limits (WAG 25-03-02 Medical FPLs).

Additional Requirements:

Individuals who are approved for Medicaid are not eligible for QI-1.

Individuals who are in met spenddown status [OR approved with DoA (met or unmet)] are not eligible for QI-1.

DoA medical is met systematically via waiver services

Individuals who are in unmet spenddown status are eligible for QI-1 because, while in unmet status, they are not considered to be receiving Medicaid.

QI-1 eligibility may be retroactive to 3 months before the month of application.
