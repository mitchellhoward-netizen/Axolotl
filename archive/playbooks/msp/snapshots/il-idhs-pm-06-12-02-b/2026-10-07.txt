PM 06-12-02-b: Dual Eligibility (QMB/Medicaid)

WAG 06-12-02-b

Medicare Part A beneficiaries are automatically eligible for QMB if they receive:

cash (non-sheltered care cases), or

regular MANG.

They may also be QMB eligible if they:

are in spenddown, or

live in a long term care or sheltered care facility.
