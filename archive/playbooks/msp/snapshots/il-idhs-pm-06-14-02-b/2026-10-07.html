PM 06-14-02-b: Dual Eligibility

WAG 06-14-02-b

A Medicare Part A beneficiary may be eligible for QI-1 and be in spenddown.

A Community Spenddown case that contains a QI-1 is not centrally canceled after the 12-month enrollment period. REDE these cases every 12 months.
