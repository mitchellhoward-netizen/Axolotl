The purpose of this Medi-Cal Eligibility Division Information Letter (MEDIL) is to provide
counties with information and reminders regarding the Qualified Medicare Beneficiary
(QMB) program for the members who do not receive Supplemental Security Income/
State Supplementary Payment (SSI/SSP). In this letter, these members are referred to
as non-SSI or non-SSP individuals.

Background
Effective January 1, 2025, California has transitioned to a Medicare Part A Buy-In State.
This transition under the Medicare Part A Buy-In agreement with the Centers for
Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) allows for eligible full-scope Medi-Cal members
who are enrolled in Medicare Part B if they qualify for the Qualified Medicare Beneficiary
(QMB) program to be automatically enrolled in Medicare Part A Buy-In by the
Department of Health Care Services (DHCS). The state will pay their Medicare Part A
premiums. Once enrolled in the Medicare Part A Buy-In, QMB Medi-Cal members will
have dual eligibility for Medicare and Medi-Cal covered services.

On September 21, 2023, CMS issued a Final Rule to streamline the enrollment and
eligibility determination processes for Medicare Savings Programs (MSPs). This 2023
CMS final rule required states to automatically enroll current SSI/SSP recipients eligible
for premium-free Medicare Part A into the QMB program starting October 1, 2024.

On October 10, 2023, Governor Newsom signed Senate Bill (SB) 311, which amended
California Welfare and Institutions Code §14005.11. This senate bill required DHCS to
enter a Medicare Part A buy-in agreement with CMS for eligible Medicare members
through a state plan amendment. Once California became a Medicare Part A buy-in
state under this agreement, DHCS auto-enrolled all eligible SSI/SSP full-scope Medi-
Cal members enrolled in Medicare Part B into the QMB program and the Medicare Part
A buy-in. This policy took effect on January 1, 2025, as part of phase two of the 2023
CMS final rule.

Qualified Medicare Beneficiary (QMB) Program
The Qualified Medicare Beneficiary (QMB) program helps cover the Medicare costs for
eligible individuals by paying for their Medicare Parts A and B premiums, deductibles,
and coinsurance fees.

To qualify for the QMB program, an individual must meet the following criteria:
• Be eligible for Medicare Parts A and B.
• Meet the net countable income requirements of below 100% of the
Federal Poverty Level (FPL).
• Meet other Medi-Cal eligibility requirements.
Starting January 1, 2025, DHCS facilitated and ensured the seamless enrollment of
all eligible SSI/SSP full-scope Medi-Cal members into the QMB program. (In
California, individuals who qualify for the SSP also receive Medi-Cal and are entitled
to the QMB program if they meet other eligibility requirements.) DHCS automatically
assigned the QMB aid code (80) to these members case records in addition to their
primary aid codes (10, 20, or 60). This implementation took place in two phases:

Phase 1: As part of the 2023 CMS Final Rule from October 1, 2024, through
December 31, 2024, DHCS automatically enrolled current SSI/SSP members
eligible for premium-free Medicare Part A into the QMB program.

Phase 2: On January 1, 2025, DHCS automatically enrolled all SSI/SSP eligible
members into the QMB program in accordance with Senate Bill 311 and the 2023
CMS Final Rule during Phase 2.

QMB Program Reminders for the Non-SSI/Non-SSP Individuals
As a reminder, non-SSI/non-SSP individuals are not automatically enrolled in the
QMB program and to qualify to the program must meet the above QMB program
eligibility criteria. To apply, they must submit an application for Medi-Cal and/or
Medicare Savings Program (MSP) at their county office.

When an eligible non-SSI/non-SSP Medi-Cal member is enrolled in Medicare Part B
and qualifies for the QMB program, the county will evaluate their QMB eligibility.
Following the Medi-Cal Eligibility Data System (MEDS) update, DHCS will request
Medicare Part A buy-in to CMS.

The existing QMB Approval Notice of Action (NOA) will notify non-SSI/Non-SSP
QMB Medi-Cal members about their enrollment into the QMB program. These
individuals will also receive the Medicare Part A buy-in approval letter from the
Social Security Administration (SSA). (An example of an SSA approval letter is
enclosed). For more information about the effective date of Medicare Part A, please
refer to ACWDL 24-20.

QMB Program Effective Date for Non-SSI/Non-SSP Medi-Cal Members
The SSA may contact county offices to verify the Medi-Cal eligibility of Medi-Cal
members applying for Medicare Part B. Once a Medi-Cal member’s eligibility is
confirmed, the SSA will initiate their Medicare Part B buy-in on the first date they
become eligible for Medicare Part B.

For example, if Ms. Sally becomes eligible for both Medicare Part B and Medi-Cal in
January 2025, her Medicare Part B buy-in will start March 1, 2025. Therefore, her
QMB eligibility and Part A Buy-In will become effective on April 1, 2025. There are
no enrollment periods for Medicare Part B. Individuals can go to SSA to apply for
Part B throughout the year as long as they have current Medi-Cal eligibility.

Additionally, as a result of the Medicare Part A agreement, Medicare Part A
entitlement can be established at any time of the year, regardless of Medicare
enrollment periods, with no premium increases due to late enrollment.

County Responsibilities Reminders
As a reminder, counties are responsible for assisting applicants/Medi-Cal members
with general questions about Medi-Cal and the Medicare Savings Programs (MSPs).

Counties shall continue to:
• Complete a Medi-Cal and/or Medicare Savings Program (MSP) application for
a Non-SSI/Non-SSP individual as outlined in ACWDL 08-21. The counties
MSP eligibility process will not change as a result of Part A buy-in accretion for
non-SSI/non-SSP individuals.
• Verify the Medi-Cal member’s Part B enrollment through MEDS or other
verification of Medicare enrollment from SSA.
• Provide members with appropriate contact information for their local SSA
office for questions related to their SSI/SSP benefits or Medicare.
Reporting Buy-In Problems for Non-SSI/Non-SSP Medi-Cal Members
As stated in MEDIL I 24-17, counties will continue to receive MEDS alerts if a buy-in
issue occurs for non-SSI/non-SSP QMB members. Counties shall continue to submit
a Problem Report to the Third-Party Liability and Recovery Division (TPLRD) as
outlined in MEPM 15F-17 for Medicare Part A buy-in-related issues for non-SSI/non-
SSP QMB members.

ACWDL 20-26 provides the current process for submitting a State Medicare Buy-In
Problem Report.
