PM 06: Special Programs

PM 06-12-00: Qualified Medicare Beneficiary (QMB) Program

PM 06-12-06: Ending QMB

WAG 06-12-06

End QMB benefits when a client:

loses Part A entitlement (e.g., a disabling medical condition for a person under 65 improves);

no longer meets financial or nonfinancial requirements; or

was incorrectly approved for QMB.

When ending QMB benefits or when a client is notified by SSA that benefits have ended, notify the SSA Programs Control Unit.
