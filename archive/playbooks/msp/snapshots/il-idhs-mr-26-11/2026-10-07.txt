MANUAL REVISIONS

Increase in the Monthly Medical Income Standards

This release provides the revised income standards for the programs listed below based on the 2026 Federal Poverty Level (FPL). The revised standards are effective 01/01/26 for all programs except Medicaid Presumptive Eligibility (MPE). The revised MPE standards are effective 03/01/2026. The revised standards will be updated in IES on 03/03/2026 effective April 2026 for active cases. For pending applications, the new standards will be applied for any budget month beginning with January 2026.

Family Assist and Family Health Spenddown Standards

See PM 15-06-01-a for Family Assist and PM 15-06-01-e for Family Health Spenddown standards. Children and pregnant women who qualify for spenddown must spend down to the Family Health Spenddown standard.

Mass Change Action in IES

IES will process a mass change action due to the 2026 income standards update. The mass change will occur in March, effective April 2026. The following changes will be certified for individuals whose countable income is below the income limit for 2026:

Family Health Spenddown to full coverage for certain children and pregnant women;

AABD spenddown to full coverage for certain AABD individuals;

QI-1 to SLIB;

QI-1 to QMB; and

SLIB to QMB.

A 360C Notice of Decision will be generated in IES.

Note: the mass update will not certify the following types of cases and these cases will appear on the MU-100/MUE-100 report:

Cases in case change mode;

Cases in redetermination mode;

Cases with a benefit mismatch; and

Cases with conversion information that has not been updated.

WAG 25 and the Program Standards Desk Aid

The Program Standards Desk Aid in WAG 25-03-02 has been updated with the revised income standards.

Home Equity Limits

Home Equity Limits have been updated in WAG 25-03-02(2).

Income Thresholds for a Tax Dependent Who May be Expected to be Required to File a Tax Return

The income amounts used to determine whether a tax dependent may be required to file a tax return using modified adjusted gross income (MAGI) budgeting in the 2026 calendar year are based on IRS rules for the 2025 tax year. See WAG 25-03-02(2) for the income threshold for the 2025 tax year.

MAGI Deduction-Contribution to a Health Savings Account (HSA)

The maximum allowable monthly MAGI deduction for HSAs for the 2025 tax year has been updated. See WAG 25-03-02(2).

Resource Limit for Medicare Savings Program (MSP)

The Medicare Savings Program (MSP) Resource Limits for 2026 have been updated in WAG 25-03-02(2).
