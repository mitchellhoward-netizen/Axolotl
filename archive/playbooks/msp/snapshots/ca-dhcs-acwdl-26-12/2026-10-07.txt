SUBJECT: UPDATED NOTICES OF ACTION (NOA) SNIPPETS FOR THE MEDICARE
SAVINGS PROGRAMS (MSP) REFLECTING ASSET REINSTATEMENT AND
PART A BUY-IN POLICY CHANGES

REFERENCES: All COUNTY WELFARE DIRECTORS LETTERS: ACWDL 08-21, ACWDL 22-25,
and ACWDL 24-20.

Purpose
The purpose of this All County Welfare Directors Letter (ACWDL) is to provide counties
with the updated Notices of Action (NOA) snippets for the Medicare Savings Programs
(MSP), including the Qualified Medicare Beneficiary (QMB), Specified Low-Income
Medicare Beneficiary (SLMB), and Qualifying Individual (QI) programs. These revisions
align with recent policy changes and are crucial for ensuring accurate and consistent
communication with members regarding their MSP eligibility.

Key Updates
Developed in cooperation with stakeholders, these updates aim to ensure clarity,
accuracy, and minimize confusion for members. The updates to the NOA language
reflect the following policy changes:

• Reinstatement of assets: Effective January 1, 2026, asset limits for all Non-
Modified Adjusted Gross Income (Non-MAGI) programs, including Medicare
Savings Programs and Long-Term Care (LTC) were reinstated (ACWDL 25-14).

• Part A Buy-in policy changes: Effective January 1, 2025, the Department of
Health Care Services (DHCS) entered into a Medicare Part A buy-in agreement
(ACWDL 24-20). This agreement allows DHCS to streamline the enrollment
process for all eligible QMB members in Medicare Part A and pay their premiums.
California as a Part A buy-in state, is required by the Center for Medicare and
Medicaid Services (CMS) Final Rule to automatically enroll Medicare eligible
Supplemental Security Income (SSI) recipients into the QMB program.

Specific NOA Snippet Updates
The enclosed NOA snippets include the following revisions:

• Approvals for QMB, SLMB, and QI programs: The updated NOA clearly
informs the member or applicant of the approval for the QMB, SLMB, or QI
program, and the program effective date.

• Discontinuance and Denial for all MSP programs: The new discontinuance and
denial language provides detailed explanations for a denial or discontinuance of
the MSP programs due to income over the program limit and not having
Medicare Part A and B. Separate discontinuance and denial NOAs have been
added to allow counties to notify members and applicants of a specific reason for
a discontinuance or denial.

Implementation and Accessibility
The English versions of all NOA snippets have been translated into the 18 threshold
languages: Arabic, Armenian, Cambodian, Chinese, Farsi, Hindi, Hmong, Japanese,
Korean, Laotian, Mien, Punjabi, Russian, Spanish, Tagalog, Thai, Ukrainian, and
Vietnamese. For reference, the updated NOA snippets, in both English and the
translated versions, are enclosed with this ACWDL. The Statewide Automated Welfare
System (SAWS) will make the necessary programming changes to incorporate the
English and translated NOA snippets in the upcoming 26.11. SAWS release.
