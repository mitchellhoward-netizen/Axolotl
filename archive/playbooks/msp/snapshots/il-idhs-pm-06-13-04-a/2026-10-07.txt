PM 06: Special Programs

PM 06-13-00: Specified Low-Income Medicare Beneficiary (SLIB) Program

PM 06-13-04: Rede

PM 06-13-04-a: SLIB Only

WAG 06-13-04-a

REDE SLIB only cases at least once every 12 months. No review of changes is required by the Family Community Resource Center during the periods between REDEs unless the information appears on the Priority Action List (PAL) as a result of the IEVS or any other crossmatch. SLIB only cases appear as "due" for REDE on the PAL, in the 11th month of the 12-month eligibility period.
