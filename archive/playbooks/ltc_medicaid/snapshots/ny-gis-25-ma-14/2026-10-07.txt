The purpose of this General Information System (GIS) message is to inform local departments of
social services of the year 2026 Medicaid regional nursing home rates which must be used when
calculating a transfer of assets penalty period for coverage of nursing facility services. This is in
accordance with 96 ADM-8, “OBRA ’93 Provisions on Transfers and Trusts” and 06 OMM/ADM-
5, “Deficit Reduction Act of 2005 – Long-Term Care Medicaid Eligibility Changes.” These rates
are based on average private pay nursing home costs in each of the seven regions in the State.
Districts must use the regional rate in effect as of the date of application or the date request for
an increase in coverage for the region in which the facility is located.
