MR #26.05 Community Spouse Maintenance Needs Allowance and Community Spouse Resource Allowance Limit Increases

01/29/2026

Summary:

Due to Public Act 102-1037, the Community Spouse Maintenance Needs Allowance (CSMNA) and Community Spouse Resource Allowance (CSRA) will increase annually. Effective 01/01/2026, the IES has been updated to reflect the updated amounts, as follows:

The CSMNA limit for 2026 is $4,066.50.

The CSRA limit for 2026 is $143,172.00.

The CSMNA and CSRA will increase annually based on the Federal Poverty Level (FPL) amounts posted by the federal government.

Reminder: The CSRA determination only occurs once. If the CSRA was already determined at application or long term care determination, then the previously determined amount will not be updated.

Please review the following policy manual and WAG references for more information: PM 07-02-22: Community Spouse Resource Allowance and WAG 15-04-04-a: Community Spouse Maintenance Needs Allowance.
