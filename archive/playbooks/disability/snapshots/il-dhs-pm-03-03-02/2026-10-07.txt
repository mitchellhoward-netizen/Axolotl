PM 03-03-00: Eligibility for Supplemental Security Income (SSI)

PM 03-03-02: SSI and AABD Cash

WAG 03-03-02.

AABD Cash payments are made to persons whose age, blindness, or disability satisfies the SSI eligibility requirements.

To receive AABD Cash, a person must:

be receiving SSI; or

have been denied SSI due to income; or

be a noncitizen who:

is 65 years of age or older, and

meets the citizenship/immigration requirements for medical assistance, and

legally resided in the U.S. on 08/22/96, and

was denied SSI due to a finding of "not disabled"; or

be a noncitizen who is ineligible for SSI due to expiration of the federal 7 year limit.

The cash payment is the difference between the client's income and the AABD Cash Assistance Standard.
