SI 01330.110 Deeming - Meeting Resource Eligibility Requirement - Policy

Authority: Act, Sec. 1614(f), as amended. Regulations 20 CFR 416.1205

An individual who is living with an ineligible spouse meets the resource eligibility
requirement if their nonexcluded resources, including the resources of the spouse,
do not exceed the resource limit for an eligible individual with a spouse.
