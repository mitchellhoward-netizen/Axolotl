Hungry kids can’t learn
join us in making healthy
school meals for all a reality
to ensure that more Illinois schools are hunger-free,
and to support our kids to grow, learn, and thrive.
HB 2365 / SB 1419

In 2023, the General Assembly passed, and the Governor signed into law, the Healthy School
Meals for All program (Public Act 103-0532), which reimburses schools that provide all students
with free, healthy meals. Amidst federal threats and rising costs for working families, funding
the program is more urgent than ever.

Free school meals support Illinois by saving families money.
Free school meals are estimated to save families $850 - $1,200 per child, per year.
32% of Illinois children facing food insecurity do not qualify for free or reduced-priced meals.

At a time when affordability is a
growing concern, free school meals
remain a dependable way to ensure
every child has a nutritious meal.

Free school meals are more than a response to the gaps in access to food created by
cuts to programs like SNAP and other federal programs. They are an immediate solution
to hunger, a component of child wellness, and in turn, an investment in our future.
