For union members and retirees

Your fund sent you here. Here’s who we are.

Axolotl works with your benefit fund to help members get what they’re owed from Medicare, Social Security and the state: lower premiums, disability checks, help with prescriptions. You text. We fill in the forms. Nothing is sent without your yes. It’s free to you.

Get a text from us

What we can help with

Your Medicare premium

If your income is under your state’s limit, the state may pay your Part B premium: $202.90 a month in 2026.

Prescriptions

Extra Help lowers what you pay for medicines. Anyone in a Medicare Savings Program gets it automatically.

Disability

If you can’t work, we help you apply for Social Security disability and send the award to your fund.

Turning 65

We help you sign up for Medicare on time, so there’s no gap and no late penalty.

How we work

Nothing goes out without your yes

We show you exactly what will be sent. You reply YES, or it doesn’t go.

Your fund doesn’t see your case

Your fund sees totals. It learns your name only if you agree, and only what it needs.

Free to you

Your fund pays us. You never pay, and we never take a cut of back pay.

A real person when it’s hard

When a case gets complicated, a care guide takes over.
