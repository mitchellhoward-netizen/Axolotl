What we can help with

Your Medicare premium

If your income is under New York’s limit, the state may pay your whole Part B premium. If your fund pays back half of it today, that stops, and you still come out about $100 a month ahead.

Prescriptions

Extra Help lowers what you pay for medicines. Anyone in a Medicare Savings Program gets it automatically.

Renewing every year

The state asks for a renewal each year. We text you before it’s due, and send it with your yes.

When something changes

If you move, your income changes or your spouse passes away, text us. We’ll tell you what it means and update it.

How we work

Nothing goes out without your yes

We show you exactly what will be sent. You reply YES, or it doesn’t go.

Your fund doesn’t see your case

Your fund sees totals. It learns your name only if you agree, and only what it needs.

Free to you

Your fund pays us. You never pay, and we never take a cut of back pay.

A real person when it’s hard

When a case gets complicated, a person on our team takes over.
