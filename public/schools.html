Get your number

Help for every family, not another letter.

Mycelium gives each family a helper by text, in their language, that gets the income form in and the benefits it unlocks used, sorts out what keeps a child home, and finishes enrollment and consents. $5.60 a student a year, and $2.40 more only if your own filings show it worked.

Today,
a letter.

A fifth absence, a missing income form, an unsigned consent: each one is already in your records, and today each one gets a letter. With Mycelium it gets an offer from the school instead: a helper that finds out what is actually in the way, the bus, the Medi-Cal renewal, the shutoff notice, the IEP meeting, and does the thing, with the family’s yes on each action.

For the offer, we act as your school official under FERPA. Everything after that runs on the family’s own consent, which is why the thread stays with them. You see attendance and completion, and patterns in aggregate, like forty families on one cut bus route; never a reason.

    First we read how you’re funded.

    Your LCAP, your attendance file and your Fall 1 count, line by line: every dollar that depends on a family finishing something. The number comes first, free.

    Then it rehearses on last year.

    Before it texts anyone, it runs on last year’s records. You see who it would have reached and what it would have said, and you decide when it’s ready.

    You offer it. The family says yes.

    A fifth absence, a missing form or an unsigned consent sends an offer from you, in the family’s language. They say yes or they don’t. Your staff see only the cases that need a person.

    You pay per student.

    $5.60 per enrolled student a year, invoiced like any other line. $2.40 more only if numbers you certify to the state move: your Fall 1 unduplicated count, or funded ADA at P-2. A fiscal-year term, no setup fee, no minimum.

    Who was offered a helper, from your own records, and whether attendance improved. Patterns in aggregate. Every rule the helper follows, in writing, before anything goes live. A monthly report your board can read.

    What you see.

    The address, the eviction notice, the immigration question, the sick grandparent. Any message, unless the family chooses to share it. Anything about a family that didn’t say yes.

    What you never see.

    The California Student Data Privacy Agreement before we see a single record. We act as your school official only to make the offer; anything sent to another agency goes on the family’s own yes. Student data never trains a model.

    Why help works where letters don’t.

    • Information about financial aid did nothing. Eight minutes of help filling out the form raised college enrollment 8 points.Bettinger, Long, Oreopoulos & Sanbonmatsu, randomized, 26,000 families
    • A letter raised food-assistance enrollment from 6% to 11%. A letter plus help applying raised it to 18%.Finkelstein & Notowidigdo, randomized, 31,000 seniors
    • Financial-aid reminders by text and email to more than 800,000 students changed nothing.Bird, Castleman, Denning, Goodman, Lamberton & Rosinger, randomized
    • A note home about an eye exam did nothing. Glasses handed out at school raised reading and math. A quarter of the children who needed them still went without, mostly because a permission form didn’t come back.Florida vision trial, randomized, 18,000 students

    No one has run this trial with families of school-age children: one helper, the family’s own, finishing the form instead of mailing it. Yours would be it, offered school by school and measured against the schools not yet offered, so by June you know.
