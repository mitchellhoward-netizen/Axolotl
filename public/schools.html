One family, counted right.

In a California district where more than 55% of students are low-income, a family that enrolls in CalFresh or Medi-Cal is matched by the state, and their student counts for LCFF. The family gets help with groceries and health coverage. The district gets funding it was already meant to have.

Every family, followed through. Nothing moves without a yes.

Mycelium keeps a live record of where each family stands on each outcome: likely eligible, contacted, agreed, filed, confirmed. It moves a family forward only when it’s sure. When it isn’t, a person on your team or ours decides.

  1. MapWe read how your district is funded and where outcomes stall: your state’s formula, your Medicaid claiming, your enrollment and attendance data.
  2. FindNames in: the students whose families are likely eligible, missing a consent or slipping on attendance.
  3. ReachYour district sends one message with our number. Families text back in English or Spanish, at any hour.
  4. YesNothing is signed or filed until the parent replies YES to exactly what will happen.
  5. ConfirmWe follow each case to the result: the consent on file, the approval, the match, the student back in class.
  6. ReportOutcomes out: a monthly ledger by school of what changed and what it’s worth, with the hard cases handed to your coordinators.

Why this isn’t done already.

Each case is small. There are thousands.

A form, a call, a ride. Your staff know how to do every one. Nobody has the hours to do them for every family, every month.

The work sits outside the school

Food benefits, health coverage and transit run through county and state offices. We work with families across all of them.

Only the family can sign

No data match can enroll anyone or give consent. We get it done with the parent, from a message they trust because it came from their school.

You pay for results

A fixed fee per outcome, agreed up front. Nothing for outreach, nothing for families who say no.

What we never do

Act without a yes

Nothing is sent, signed or filed until a parent replies YES to exactly what will happen.

Share a family’s case

The district sees outcomes and totals. It learns more only when the family agrees, and only what it needs.

Touch a student’s education

We never change an IEP, a placement, a grade or a service.

Charge families

Families never pay us. We sign your state’s student data privacy agreement before we see a single record.
