The work that
never sleeps.

This is the coordination tax: the unpaid second job of reconciling everything your family needs against everything your benefits cover, one institution at a time.

Benny spots what’s coming, matches it to your benefits, and brings you a plan, not just another reminder.

Benny, before you have to ask.

“Based on your refill schedule, your prescription runs out in six days. Your next refill needs a doctor’s visit. I found an in-network appointment before then. Would you like me to book it?”

With your approval, Benny books the visit, coordinates with the pharmacy once your clinician authorizes the refill, and follows up on coverage and billing until the prescription is ready.

“In today’s school emails, the 504 coordinator asked for a consent form and meeting times for your child’s evaluation. Shall I prepare the paperwork and a reply for you to review?”

After you review and sign where needed, Benny sends what you approve, coordinates the meeting, and follows up until the school’s decision and any agreed accommodations are documented.

“School is closed next Friday, but you’re scheduled to work. Your employer includes backup childcare. Shall I find an available option for you to approve?”

Benny checks eligibility and availability, books after your approval, and handles any required reimbursement paperwork.

“Your newborn’s health-plan enrollment isn’t confirmed, and your plan’s deadline is next week. Shall I prepare the paperwork?”

Benny gathers the required documents, submits with your approval, and follows up until the plan confirms enrollment.

“The school’s new report recommends a speech evaluation. Your plan lists covered assessments. Shall I check which providers can see your child?”

Benny checks coverage, referral requirements, and network status, helps book the evaluation with your approval, then coordinates the school paperwork and any agreed accommodations. The work doesn’t stop at the edge of your health plan.

“Your FSA’s claim deadline is next Friday. I found an unreimbursed receipt for your child’s glasses. Shall I check eligibility and prepare the claim?”

Benny checks the expense against your plan’s rules, prepares the reimbursement request, and tracks it after you approve submission.

Illustrative workflows for the benefit we’re building, using information you choose to share. Coverage and deadlines depend on your plan; medical decisions stay with your clinician.

Care is in the details

Built to be trusted with
the things that matter.

Serious responsibilities need more than a friendly face. Here’s what our approach supports today, and what has to be settled before an employer rollout.

Your approval comes first.

Our existing consent gate requires explicit approval before consequential action. A proposal is not permission to send, submit, or spend.

Permission, not a blank check.

Institution-specific access is a requirement for the employer offering. Employees should choose what Benny can connect to and what it may do.

A record. And honest limits.

We log consent and actions. Not every action can be undone. A sent message or submitted claim may need a follow-up, not an undo button.

Your life isn’t tied to a job.

Data portability and deletion controls belong in the benefits offering. Those controls are still being developed; this site doesn’t connect to employee records.

Clear boundaries with employers.

Our design requirement: employers do not see identifiable individual records. Reporting and access terms must be agreed before any rollout.

Security review before rollout.

SOC 2 status, a DPA, and company security requirements need to be verified in review. We’re not claiming SOC 2 certification or a completed enterprise review here.

Benny is AI-powered, but medical, legal, and coverage decisions stay with the qualified people and institutions responsible for them.
