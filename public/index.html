For schools, benefit funds and the people they serve

When the people you serve do better, your budget does too.

Every day a student misses, the school loses funding. Every family that never enrolls in food assistance is a student the school isn’t paid for. Every retiree who never applies for help is a premium the fund pays itself. These outcomes matter to the people you serve and to your budget, and nobody has the hours to do the work with each person. Mycelium gives each person a helper: an AI agent you sponsor, that works for them, in their language. You pay when the outcome happens.

Their outcomes are your budget.

What a school is funded for, and what a fund pays out, depends on what happens in people’s lives. Your staff’s work stops at your door. In between are outcomes the people you serve want, and that bring money in or keep it from going out.

Why it doesn’t happen. Stranded alignment.

We call it stranded alignment: outcomes an institution and the people it serves both want, that never happen because reaching them costs more than anyone can spend. Each case is small and sits between offices, so it’s nobody’s job. A typical school case manager carries 110 to 125 students.

Letters and portals

Tell people what to do. They reach everyone and change little, because information alone rarely moves anyone.

Coordinators and case managers

Do the work with people, and it works. But one person can only carry so many families.

A helper for every person

Does the work with each person, as often as it takes, for everyone you serve. That’s new.

What changed: in one experiment, eight minutes of help filling out the federal student aid form raised college enrollment from 34% to 42%. Information alone did nothing. An AI agent can now spend those eight minutes with every family.

Case manager loads: a study funded by the National Institute of Justice. Student aid form: the H&R Block experiment by Bettinger, Long, Oreopoulos and Sanbonmatsu, summarized by J-PAL.

How it works. Nothing moves without a yes.

Mycelium works inside your institution and out in people’s lives. It learns what you need from your staff, and does the work with each person.

  1. LearnWe work with your staff to learn your goals, your rules and how you’re funded, and connect to the systems you already use.
  2. OfferNames in: your own records show who could use help. You send one message offering a helper, from a name people already trust.
  3. HelpEach person’s helper works with them by text or WhatsApp, in their language, and with the agencies and offices around them.
  4. YesNothing is filed, signed or shared until the person says yes to exactly what will happen.
  5. ReportOutcomes out: you see results, not people’s lives. Hard cases go to your staff.
  6. SeeWhen forty families hit the same problem, you hear about it. One family’s problem is a case. Forty is a policy decision.

A helper that belongs to the person, sponsored by you.

Personal AI will reach most people the way financial advisors and good lawyers did: the people with the most friction last. Institutions can change that. The helper you sponsor works for the person, on anything making life harder, and on your goals only where they agree. You bring four things no app store can.
