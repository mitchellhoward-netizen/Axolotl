3:40 PMAt the school gate

Every family makes Lincoln easier for the next one.

Every time Axolotl gets something done at a school, it learns what worked: which office, which form, how long it took. The school checks it and makes it official. The next family just says yes. Nobody’s personal details go into it.

  2. Many familiesIt keeps working, so it becomes proven.

What you share here.

What you send us

We collect the email address and message you submit so we can reply. Inquiries are stored in our database. Please do not send your child’s records, health information or school passwords.

The pilot list and school requests

A family or circle signup gives us your phone number, and a circle signup also says how many families and, if you want, your school. A school pilot request gives us your name, role, school or district and email. All of it is stored in our database. A text provider may process your phone number to send a confirmation text.

This website is not the service

These forms do not connect to your school or to your child's records. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser makes requests to Google and to our hosting provider when you visit. How the service itself handles your family's information is in the privacy policy.
