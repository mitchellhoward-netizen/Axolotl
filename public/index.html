We lost our apartment and we're staying with my sister across town. Can he keep going to his school? McKinney-Vento Act: school stability for kids without steady housing Finds the district’s transportation contact, requests the ride in writing, and follows up until the route is confirmed.
His teacher says his reading is behind. I don't know what I'm supposed to ask for. IDEA: evaluations for special education Drafts your written request for an evaluation, sends it on your yes, and records the date the district's clock started.
The meeting was all in English and I didn't understand most of it. Title VI, Civil Rights Act: communication in a language you understand Asks the school in writing for an interpreter and translated documents before the next meeting.
We're paying full price for lunch and I think we qualify for help. National School Lunch Program: free and reduced-price meals Fills the application, shows you exactly what it will send, and files it on your yes.
We agreed in the meeting that she gets extra time. It's not happening in class. Her IEP or 504 plan: accommodations the school agreed to Writes to the school quoting what the plan says, and keeps following up until they confirm it's in place.

The limits, before you find them yourself.

The honest list. The rest of this page only means something if this part is true.

It won't send anything without your yes.

Every email, form and submission waits for an explicit yes from you. A suggestion is not permission.

It tells you what it can't open.

A sign-in wall, a form that only exists on paper, a district that wants a phone call. It names the wall instead of guessing.

It won't pretend a form went through.

Done means the school's own confirmation came back. If it can't confirm, it says so and gives you the link.

It can't see your school portal.

It never asks for your password. If the portal matters, you sign in yourself and it only reads.

It's strongest at reading and research.

A clean form it has handled before is reliable. A complicated new district form may come back to you as a link.

It isn't a doctor, a lawyer or the school.

It helps you through the process. Decisions about your child stay with you, your school and your providers.

Your family's information is yours. It is never sold. The only system that reads your messages is the AI that writes the replies.

What you share here.

What you send us

We collect the email address and message you submit so we can reply. Inquiries are stored in our database. Please do not send your child’s records, health information or school passwords.

The pilot list and school requests

A family or circle signup gives us your phone number, and a circle signup also says how many families and, if you want, your school. A school pilot request gives us your name, role, school or district and email. All of it is stored in our database. A text provider may process your phone number to send a confirmation text.

This website is not the service

These forms do not connect to your school or to your child's records. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser makes requests to Google and to our hosting provider when you visit. How the service itself handles your family's information is in the privacy policy.
