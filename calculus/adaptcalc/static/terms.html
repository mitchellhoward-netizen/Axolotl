Terms of use

What this is

Marginalia is a pilot: a working book, offered to a small number of families while it is improved. It is a study aid for families who direct their children’s learning, not an accredited school or a substitute for a teacher’s judgment.

How checking works, and its limits

Answers and steps are checked with a computer algebra system, and handwriting is read by an AI model. Reading handwriting can go wrong. If a problem is marked wrong and you believe it is right, check the transcript shown in the feedback; for diagnostic questions, use “recheck”. The progress estimates are estimates.

Your account

You are responsible for the account and for the learners you add. Keep your password private. One family per account, up to eight learners. Daily limits on photos, questions and packets keep the service affordable for everyone.

The book’s content

Kindergarten to grade 5 lessons are from Marginalia Elementary Mathematics, written for Marginalia. Pre-Algebra and Algebra 1 lesson text is adapted from Prealgebra 2e and Elementary Algebra 2e by Lynn Marecek, MaryAnne Anthony-Smith and Andrea Honeycutt Mathis, published by OpenStax under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 license, used from the releases made under that license. Access the original for free at openstax.org. OpenStax is not affiliated with and does not endorse Marginalia. Generated practice problems, the skill map, and the software are ours.

Ending

You can delete your account at any time from the Learners page. We may end the pilot; if we do, we will give account holders notice and a chance to download their data first.
