Privacy notice

Marginalia is a math textbook that checks a child’s written work. This notice says what we keep, why, and who else sees it. It is written to be read, not skimmed past.

Who holds the account

A parent or guardian creates the account and adds learners. Children do not sign up, do not give an email address, and do not have usernames or profiles visible to anyone else. A learner is a first name chosen by the parent.

What we keep

  • Your email address, your name if you give it, and a scrambled (hashed) form of your password.
  • For each learner: the first name you entered, the course, the starting point you chose, the packets made for them, the photos of their pages, what was read from those photos, how each problem was checked, the questions they asked about their work and the notes given back, and the model’s estimate of each skill.
  • A count of how many photos, questions and packets each learner used each day (to keep costs bounded).

We do not collect location, contacts, advertising identifiers, or anything about the learner beyond their math work. There are no advertisements and no tracking scripts. Fonts and the math typesetting are served from this site, so your browser does not contact other companies when you read a page.

Who else sees the work

Two services help check the work. They receive only what they need for that one check:

  • Anthropic (Claude) reads the photo of a page and turns the handwriting into text, and writes the margin notes when a learner asks a question. Anthropic’s commercial terms say it does not train its models on this data.
  • TypeSafe (Jev) answers narrow questions about the checked work, for example which listed mistake best explains a line, and whether a generated sentence brings in an idea the book has not taught. It does not receive names, emails or the learner’s history.

The site runs on Railway, which stores the data. We do not sell or share data with anyone else, and we do not use it for anything except running the book for you.

Your choices

  • Download everything we hold about your learners from the Learners page (“Download my data”).
  • Remove a learner to delete their pages, photos and progress immediately.
  • Delete the account to remove everything at once. Deleted data is not kept.

Children’s privacy

Because a parent or guardian creates the account and decides what to share, and because we collect only the child’s first name and math work for the purpose of teaching, we treat all learner data as belonging to the family. If you think a child’s information was added without a parent’s consent, write to us and we will delete it.

Questions

Write to the address you received your access code from. This notice may change as the pilot grows; the date below will change with it, and we will tell account holders about anything significant.
